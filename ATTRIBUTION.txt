The original still-image bytes are preserved without edits. The local interface
scales them for display. This handbook adds a new independent interface and a
Chinese reading guide; it is not an official LemoLab product. Invocation
templates are editorial additions and are labeled separately from original
style prompts.

Third-party material retains its original licenses. See the saved source
README.zh-CN.md and each source/styles/<slug>/demo/CREDITS file. Attribution and
other license files nested in the original demo folders are retained as well.
The handbook does not download, redistribute, or embed the demo videos/audio.

The original README excludes three voice recordings from CC BY 4.0:
Game Show Flat's macOS say shouts; Paper-cut Lightbox's Edge TTS narration; and
Broadcast News Package's Doubao TTS narration. None are included here.
